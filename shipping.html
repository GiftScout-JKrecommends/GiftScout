Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

GiftScout primarily provides digital gift recommendation services. No physical products are sold or shipped directly by GiftScout unless explicitly stated.

Delivery Method

Your personalized gift recommendations will be delivered through one of the following methods:

  • WhatsApp
  • Email
  • Any other communication method agreed upon during your order.

Delivery Time

Once payment is confirmed and all required details are received, recommendations are typically delivered within 24–48 hours.

Delays

In rare situations, delivery may be delayed due to incomplete information provided by the customer or unforeseen circumstances. If this happens, we will inform you as soon as possible.

Physical Products

GiftScout only recommends products. We do not manufacture, stock, or ship products purchased from third-party websites or sellers. Shipping timelines, charges, and delivery are governed by the respective seller's policies.
