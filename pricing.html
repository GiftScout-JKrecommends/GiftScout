Pricing Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

GiftScout provides personalized gift recommendation services based on your specific requirements, including the recipient, occasion, budget, interests, and preferred gifting date. Our pricing is transparent, simple, and automatically calculated based on your selected gift budget.

How Our Pricing Works

Our service fee is calculated as 10% of your selected gift budget, subject to the following limits:

  • Minimum Service Fee: ₹10
  • Maximum Service Fee: ₹999
  • Otherwise: 10% of your selected gift budget.

What's Included

Your service fee covers:

  • Personalized gift research
  • Thoughtfully curated gift recommendations
  • Recommendations tailored to your recipient, occasion, and interests
  • Suggestions within your specified budget
  • Revision of recommendations where appropriate, as outlined in our Cancellation & Refund Policies

Payment

Payment must be completed before our team begins preparing your personalized recommendations. Once payment has been successfully received, our research process starts immediately.

Changes to Pricing

GiftScout reserves the right to revise its pricing policy or service fees at any time. Any changes will apply only to new orders placed after the updated pricing becomes effective.
