Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

GiftScout provides personalized digital gift recommendation services. Since every recommendation is researched and customized based on the information you provide, refunds are generally not available once the recommendations have been delivered.

Delivery Timeline

After successful payment and receipt of all required information, your personalized gift recommendations will typically be delivered within 24–48 hours.

If your recommendations are not delivered within 24–48 hours, you may contact us for assistance. If we are unable to fulfill your order within a reasonable time, you may be eligible for a full refund.

If You Don't Like the Recommendations

Our recommendations are carefully curated based on the preferences, budget, occasion, and details you provide.

  • We encourage you to tell us what you didn't like.
  • We will provide one complimentary revision with alternative recommendations based on your feedback.
  • As this is a personalized digital service involving research and time, dissatisfaction with the recommendations alone does not qualify for a refund once the recommendations have been delivered.
